About the Institute

Sovereign technology for a connected world.

ICCC is a research and knowledge-exchange institute operating at the intersection of cyber-defence, cross-border intellectual property, and collaborative technology. We connect academic rigour with operational expertise across EMEA and Ukraine.

Our work is rooted in the belief that technological sovereignty and international collaboration are not opposing forces — they are mutually reinforcing conditions for a secure, prosperous digital future.

We partner with governments, defence agencies, universities, and private-sector innovators to design frameworks that protect critical infrastructure, enable lawful technology transfer, and multiply the value of shared research.

From legal interoperability studies to joint R&D programmes, ICCC provides the institutional infrastructure — and the trusted networks — that allow sovereign entities to collaborate without compromising integrity.

Operational Framework

How We Work

Legal & Regulatory Alignment

We map the legal interfaces between EMEA and Ukrainian jurisdictions, producing actionable frameworks for IP licensing, data transfer, and joint venture formation. Every engagement begins with a jurisdiction-specific compliance review.

Technical Due Diligence

Our technical directors assess the security posture, infrastructure resilience, and interoperability of partner technologies. Findings are documented against ISO 27001 and NIST baselines, with remediation pathways where required.

Secure Knowledge Transfer

We design and operate secure channels for technology exchange — including encrypted documentation vaults, cleared-personnel protocols, and staged IP release schedules — ensuring that sensitive knowledge moves only where it is lawfully permitted to go.

Joint R&D Facilitation

ICCC convenes academic and industrial partners around shared research agendas. We provide the governance structures, IP-sharing agreements, and project management scaffolding that let sovereign entities co-develop without ceding control.
